SchemeWise
An intelligent scheme-matching platform designed to help marginalized entrepreneurs discover relevant government welfare and financial assistance schemes based on their eligibility and profile.
About the Project
SchemeWise aims to simplify the process of discovering schemes by helping users identify options that may be relevant to their profile and eligibility.
